✦ Our Core Mission & Values ✦

Why We Do This

Mid Michigan Drift Club exists for one reason: to get people drifting. Run by drivers with over 10 years of motorsport experience, we're an intensely value-driven organization — every driver, spectator, and sponsor should walk away feeling like their experience was worth far more than the cost.

By reinvesting our revenue directly back into track facilities, safety, and driver development, we've built an unshakeable bond with an exceptionally loyal automotive community.

What we run is a complete pathway — from a driver's first clutch kick on a Driver Advancement Day, to high-level competitive tandem drifting under the lights.
