Our Core Mission & Values

Why We Do This

Mid Michigan Drift Club exists for one reason: to get people drifting. Run by drivers with over a decade of drifting experience, we're an intensely value-driven organization — every driver, spectator, and sponsor should walk away feeling like their experience was more than worth it.

We focus our reinvestment on what's most valuable: the drivers. Track additions, safety equipment, and driver development opportunities. It's how we've built an unshakeable bond with an exceptionally loyal automotive community.

What we run is a complete pathway — from a driver's first clutch kick at a clinic to the final round of the Arroyo Grand Nationals.

01

Why Partner With MMDC?

The Arroyo Grand Nationals is a party-tire competition series — and it's going to be legit. It's where our 2027 effort goes: the rounds we film, the events we promote, and the stage your brand is on.

🏆

The Format

  • Three-Part Series Two rounds a day at every event, forward and reverse layouts — six rounds across the season.
  • Daily & Series Winners A winner every day, and a series champion at the final round.
  • 20–24 Driver Field A full, consistent bracket with a Top 16.
  • Equalized Spec Tire A max 235-wide rear, led by the Arroyo Grand Sports A/S, levels the field and keeps driver costs low.
🎟️

The Field & The Event

  • 16 Season Passes Awarded by application — the best drivers we can find, committed for the season.
  • 8 Wild Cards Open to drivers on our approved wild-card list.
  • An Event To Be Seen Food trucks, a DJ, and a crowd. It's still an MMDC event.

Flagship Events & Festivals

  • Season Highlights Opening Day, Spring Matsuri, Weekend of Michigan Drifting, Doubletime, and Friendsgiving.
  • Nightshift Team-focused night drifting built around vehicle style, liveries, and team driving dynamics.

Development & Inclusion

  • 4 Clinic Days The foundation of the club — relaxed, focused sessions equipping new drivers with essential technique.
  • 2 Ladies Days Dedicated events accelerating skill development for women in motorsport.
📍

Our Tracks

  • Jackson Speedway Our home. A rowdy short track, with 400–800 people at a typical event.
  • Owosso Speedway Concrete. A million laps and party trains.
  • Gingerman Raceway High-thrill road-course drifting.
