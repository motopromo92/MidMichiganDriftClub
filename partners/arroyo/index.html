✦ The Proposal ✦

Your Name On The Series

Mid Michigan Drift Club is inviting Arroyo to take title partnership of the Arroyo Grand Nationals for the 2027 season — three standalone competition days that sit at the top of our calendar and draw the strongest regional drivers we run in front of.

The series already carries the name. We ran the preview round on October 25, 2026 at Jackson Speedway as a drivers-and-crew competition day, and the 2027 season builds it out to three full rounds. This proposal is about what a formal partnership adds to that foundation.

✦ 01 ✦

Why This Isn't Banner Advertising

🛞

The Spec Tire Is The Series

  • Every Car, Every Bracket The Grand Nationals run an equalized spec: a max 235-wide Arroyo Grand Sport A/S. Competing means running your product.
  • Product Under Load, On Camera Tandem drifting is the hardest test a street tire sees. Every pass is a demonstration, not an impression.
  • Cost Control Is The Sell The spec keeps driver spend down, which is exactly why the field accepts it — the tire is the reason the series is affordable to enter.
🔬

A Development Channel, Not Just A Logo

  • Private Test Days Jackson private-day rentals give Arroyo a closed environment for compound and construction testing with real drivers.
  • Direct Driver Feedback A 24-driver competition field is a standing test panel that will tell you exactly how the tire behaves.
  • Media Creation The same days double as controlled shoots for Arroyo's own channels.

✦ 02 ✦

Series Architecture

🏆

Three Competition Days

  • Standalone Rounds Distributed across the 2027 calendar rather than bolted onto open drift days.
  • Top Regional Field The rounds pull the strongest drivers in our region and beyond.
⚔️

Bracket Format

  • 24-Driver Field Qualifying into two full Top 16 tandem brackets in a single day.
  • High-Stakes Tandem Head-to-head battles, which is the footage that travels.
📍

Where It Runs

  • Jackson Speedway Home base at 3363 Page Ave, Jackson, Michigan.
  • Owosso & Gingerman The wider MMDC season adds a major Owosso date and an annual Gingerman event.

What The Partnership Carries

Series
Naming
  • Title naming rights across all three 2027 competition rounds.
  • Series name carried in event listings, entry pages, and results.
  • Spec-tire designation stated in the series rules.
Trackside
On Site
  • Premium banner placement in primary broadcast camera zones.
  • Reserved 12'×15' vendor booth in high-traffic spectator areas.
  • Start-line and podium branding on competition days.
Broadcast
Live Stream & Digital
  • Guaranteed 3-minute airtime in every series livestream and recap video.
  • Dedicated segments — Tech Talks, driver interviews, trackside coverage.
  • Tagging across all MMDC social output.
Fleet & Apparel
Club Assets
  • Logo on official club vehicles — drift cars, tow rigs, trailers, safety fleet.
  • Co-branded apparel release, "MMDC × Arroyo".
Commercial terms are open. Scope, term length, and any product or contingency component are all on the table — this page is the shape of the partnership, not a fixed package.
