✦ The Proposal ✦

Your Name On The Series

Mid Michigan Drift Club is inviting Arroyo to take title partnership of the Arroyo Grand Nationals for the 2027 season — three standalone competition days that sit at the top of our calendar and draw the strongest regional drivers we run in front of.

The series already carries the name. We ran the preview round on October 25, 2026 at Jackson Speedway as a drivers-and-crew competition day, and the 2027 season builds it out to three full rounds. This proposal is about what a formal partnership adds to that foundation.

✦ 01 ✦

Why This Isn't Banner Advertising

🛞

The Spec Tire Is The Series

  • Arroyo Leads The Spec The series rules cap the rear tire at 235 and name the Arroyo Grand Sports A/S first on a short approved list. The series already carries the name.
  • Exclusivity Is The Ask The rules currently permit two alternates alongside it. A title partnership is the natural route to making Arroyo the sole spec tire of the Grand Nationals.
  • Product Under Load, On Camera Tandem drifting is the hardest test a street tire sees — and a battle must be finished on the tires it started on. No mid-battle changes.
  • Cost Control Is The Sell The spec keeps driver spend down, which is exactly why the field accepts it.
🔬

A Development Channel, Not Just A Logo

  • Private Test Days Jackson private-day rentals give Arroyo a closed environment for compound and construction testing with real drivers.
  • Direct Driver Feedback A 16-driver competition field is a standing test panel that will tell you exactly how the tire behaves.
  • Media Creation The same days double as controlled shoots for Arroyo's own channels.

✦ 02 ✦

Series Architecture

🏆

Three Competition Days

  • Standalone Rounds Distributed across the 2027 calendar rather than bolted onto open drift days.
  • Top Regional Field The rounds pull the strongest drivers in our region and beyond.
⚔️

Bracket Format

  • 16-Driver Field, Two Brackets Two full Top 16 tandem brackets in a day, each on a different layout — forward and reverse — with its own practice and qualifying.
  • Qualifying Out Of 100 Line 40, angle 30, style 30. Best of two runs counts.
  • Single Elimination Traditional bracket with a battle for third. The second bracket starts clean — no carryover from the first.
📍

Where It Runs

  • Jackson Speedway Home base at 3363 Page Ave, Jackson, Michigan.
  • Owosso & Gingerman The wider MMDC season adds a major Owosso date and an annual Gingerman event.
