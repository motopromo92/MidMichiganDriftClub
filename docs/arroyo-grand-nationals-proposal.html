Your Name On The Series

Mid Michigan Drift Club invites Arroyo to take title partnership of the Arroyo Grand Nationals for the 2027 season — three standalone competition days sitting at the top of our calendar, drawing the strongest regional drivers we run in front of.

The series already carries the name — we ran the preview round on 25 October 2026 at Jackson Speedway. This proposal is what a formal partnership adds to that foundation.

WHY THIS ISN'T BANNER ADVERTISING

The rules cap the rear tire at 235 and name the Arroyo Grand Sports A/S first on a short approved list. What a title partnership adds is sole spec-tire status — and with it, every pass in every bracket as a demonstration under load rather than an impression.

The Case

Why Spec Status Matters

  • On The Car, Not Behind It Spec status puts the product on the competing cars rather than on a board in the background.
  • Hardest Possible Test The rules require a battle to be finished on the tires it began on.
  • Exclusivity Is The Ask The rules currently permit two alternates. Sole designation is the deliverable on the table.

A Development Channel

  • Private Test Days Jackson rentals give a closed environment for compound and construction testing.
  • Standing Test Panel A 16-driver competition field that will tell you exactly how the tire behaves.
  • Media Creation The same days double as controlled shoots for Arroyo's own channels.

Series & Package

Series Architecture

  • 3 Competition Days — standalone rounds distributed across the 2027 calendar, not bolted onto open drift days.
  • 16-Driver Field, Two Brackets — two full Top 16 brackets in a day, one on the forward layout and one on the reverse, each with its own practice and qualifying.
  • Qualifying Out Of 100 — line 40, angle 30, style 30, best of two runs. Single elimination, with a battle for third.
  • Equalised Spec Tire — a max 235-wide rear led by the Arroyo Grand Sports A/S levels the field and keeps entry costs low.
  • Three Venues — Jackson Speedway as series home, with the wider MMDC season adding Owosso and Gingerman dates.

What The Partnership Carries

SERIES
  • Title naming rights across all three 2027 rounds.
  • Name carried in listings, entry pages, and results.
  • Sole spec-tire designation written into the series rules.
TRACKSIDE
  • Premium banners in primary broadcast camera zones.
  • Reserved 12'×15' vendor booth in high-traffic areas.
  • Start-line and podium backdrop branding.
BROADCAST
  • Guaranteed 3-min airtime in every series stream and recap.
  • Dedicated segments — Tech Talks, driver interviews.
  • Tagging across all MMDC social output.
FLEET
  • Logo on club vehicles — drift cars, tow rigs, trailers, safety fleet.
  • Co-branded apparel release, "MMDC × Arroyo".
  • Product sampling and contingency options.
  • Named prize purse — podium prizes are currently club merchandise.
COMMERCIAL TERMS ARE OPEN.
Scope, term length, product volume, and any contingency component are all on the table. This document is the shape of the partnership, not a fixed package.
