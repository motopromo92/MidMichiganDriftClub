WHO WE ARE

✦ 01 ✦

Who We Are

Mid Michigan Drift Club sits at the front of grassroots drifting in Michigan, running high-energy, action-packed competitive motorsport events across three tracks. We cultivate an inclusive, community-driven ecosystem that bridges the gap between grassroots enthusiasts and competitive motorsport.

Built on raw passion, tire smoke, and high-horsepower excitement, the club offers an authentic connection to a dedicated and rapidly growing audience of drivers, mechanics, fans, and automotive consumers.

MMDC exists for one reason: to get people drifting. Run by drivers with over 10 years of motorsport experience, we are an intensely value-driven organization — every driver, spectator, and partner should walk away feeling their experience was worth far more than its cost. By reinvesting revenue directly into track facilities, safety, and driver development, we have built an unshakeable bond with an exceptionally loyal automotive community.

OUR MISSION

To elevate Midwest drifting culture through structured competitive series, skill development clinics, and inclusive community initiatives — delivering unmatched real-world exposure for brand partners.

Why Partner With MMDC

Driver-First Reinvestment

Event revenue funds continuous facility upgrades, safety, and driver coaching. Partners are received as support for the scene, not as advertising bolted onto it.

A Loyal, Engaged Audience

A community that actively supports the brands that support the club — drivers, crew, and spectators who buy parts, tires, fluids, and tools.

Exclusive R&D Access

Private testing days give direct, high-value product testing and media creation environments with real drivers under real load.

SEASON LINEUP

✦ 02 ✦

The 2027 Season

An expansive season of multi-day spectacles, targeted skill development, and focused community initiatives across premier Michigan venues.

Premier Events & Competitions

  • Opening Day — the official season kickoff, setting the momentum and tone for the 2027 series.
  • Spring Matsuri (2-Day Festival) — a multi-day drifting celebration featuring non-stop track action and high driver turnout.
  • Weekend of Michigan Drifting (3-Day Extravaganza) — our marquee showcase, drawing regional talent and major spectator crowds.
  • Doubletime (2-Day Competition) — intense tandem action and high-stakes competitive grassroots drifting.
  • Friendsgiving — the end-of-season community celebration and driver appreciation event.
  • Arroyo Grand Nationals — 3 Competition Days — standalone competitive rounds on an equalized spec tire, drawing top regional drivers.

Community Development & Inclusive Growth

4 Grassroots Clinics

Focused on foundational driver development for beginners, complete with tailored coaching and track support for advanced drivers.

2 Ladies Days

Dedicated events designed to empower women in motorsport, accelerating skill development in an encouraging community environment.

Private track days. Jackson Speedway rentals run outside the public calendar for product testing, media shoots, and high-intensity driver seat time — the highest-value environment we can offer a technical partner.

HOW IT WORKS

✦ 04 ✦

Brand Integration

A partnership with MMDC extends beyond the competition series. We integrate your brand across every touchpoint of our operations, for year-round visibility and direct engagement with the community.

On-Site Activation

Priority vendor row space, trackside signage, custom barrier wraps, and podium backdrop branding at Jackson, Owosso, and Gingerman.

Digital & Media Exposure

Dedicated social feature campaigns, driver spotlights, website placement, and brand integration across official event livestreams.

Product Integration & Testing

Direct access to drivers and mechanics for product sampling, real-world testing, giveaways, and contingency sponsorship programmes.

VIP & Community Access

Hot-lap experiences for brand representatives, staff appreciation access, and tailored hospitality at major multi-day events.
