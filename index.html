Michigan Grassroots Drift Events — Jackson, Owosso, and Gingerman

Mid Michigan Drift Club runs a full grassroots drift season in Michigan across three tracks from spring through fall. Whether you're a longtime car enthusiast or just looking for something wild to do on a weekend near Jackson, MI, start with the schedule above. General admission is $10, and 12 and under get in free, with food on site & free parking. Some events are run as drivers-and-crew-only track days with no spectators. Check the schedule and event page for details.

Our events range from beginner-friendly drift clinics to full open competition days, making MMDC the perfect entry point for anyone curious about grassroots motorsport in Michigan. Jackson Speedway sits at 3363 Page Ave in Jackson, MI — an easy drive from Lansing, Ann Arbor, Detroit, Grand Rapids, Kalamazoo, Battle Creek, and Flint, and most of the lower peninsula in between.
