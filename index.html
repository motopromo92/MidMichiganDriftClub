Michigan Grassroots Drift Events — Jackson, Owosso and Gingerman

Mid Michigan Drift Club runs a full grassroots drift season in Michigan, from the March clinic through to mid November, across three tracks. Whether you're a longtime car enthusiast or just looking for something wild to do on a weekend near Jackson, MI, start with the schedule above. Admission is not the same at every event. General admission is $10, and 12 and under get in free, with free parking and food on site. Not every date is open to spectators — clinics and some competition days run as drivers-and-crew-only track days. The schedule above says which is which.

Our events range from beginner-friendly drift clinics to full open competition days, making MMDC the perfect entry point for anyone curious about grassroots motorsport in Michigan. Jackson Speedway sits at 3363 Page Ave in Jackson, MI — an easy drive from Lansing, Ann Arbor, Detroit, Grand Rapids, Kalamazoo, Battle Creek and Flint, and most of the lower peninsula in between.

What It Takes To Run With Us

Drivers and spectators go through two different processes — different tickets, different waivers, different requirements. This page is the driver side. Read the Tech Book before your first event; everything below is the short version of it.

01Read the Tech Book. It is the rulebook your car gets checked against. Nothing else on this page replaces it.
02Get the car sorted. The five things below are what tech actually looks for.
03Buy a driver ticket — or check the schedule first. Entries are capped and events do sell out; every event has a different cap, some as low as 10 to 15 drivers.
04Show up and pass tech. Bring your crew — most driver entries include a crew pass.

✦ The Short Version

Recommended Equipment

What to buy, and roughly what to spend. This is guidance, not the rulebook — the Tech Book is what your car is measured against, and it wins wherever the two differ.

Welded Diff

Cheap, or free if you can weld

Both wheels have to break traction together. Weld it, or run a two-way. If you turn up with a clean diff and a hundred bucks, someone at the track will usually weld it for you.

Coilovers

Expect around $1,200 for a good set

You can spend less. A $600 set will get you on track and you will feel the difference the first time you push it. Budget properly here before anywhere else.

A Seat That Fits You

Get a bucket — used is fine

It does not need to be new or expensive. It needs to hold you in place and take the right belts. A stock seatbelt with a bucket seat is fine.

A Working Handbrake

Stock can work — depends on the car

It has to lock the rears while you are sideways. 240s have good factory brakes. E36 and E46 are decent. E90s need a 4:1 actuator. A dual-caliper setup is ideal but not required.

Helmet

Non-negotiable

Check the Tech Book for the current accepted ratings before you buy, so you do not buy twice.

Fire Extinguisher & Mounts

Mounted, not loose

It has to be secured and reachable from the driver’s seat. Loose in the footwell does not pass.

Not sure what fits your car? Ask us. We would rather talk you through it before you spend the money than fail you at tech on the morning — and we can often get parts through our distributor accounts. Message us on any of the channels in the Social menu.

✦ Michigan's Grassroots Drift Scene ✦

About MMDC

✦ Our Story

Built for the Love of the Slide

Mid Michigan Drift Club is a Michigan grassroots drift community — and we run it the right way. No velvet ropes, no gatekeeping. Just real cars, real drivers, and real people standing at a fence watching something they've never seen this close before.

We run a full season, most of it at Jackson Speedway, just off Page Ave in Jackson, MI — an easy drive from Lansing, Ann Arbor, Detroit, Grand Rapids, Kalamazoo, Battle Creek or Flint. We also run dates at Owosso Speedway in Ovid and Gingerman Raceway in South Haven. Whether you're coming to watch for the first time or you've been chasing angle for years, there's a place for you here.

For Spectators

You've Never Seen Anything Like This

Drifting is the loudest, smokiest, most sideways thing happening in Michigan on a weekend — and it's one of the best-value days out you'll find. At the events we open to the public, general admission is $10 at the gate and 12 and under get in free. Walk the paddock, get close to the cars, and watch drivers push the limits in ways that will genuinely surprise you.

✦ First Time At A Drift Event?

Here's What to Expect

01

Show Up & Park

Free parking at Jackson Speedway. At events with general admission it's $10 at the gate, free for 12 and under, with no pre-registration needed. Check the event first — not every date is open to spectators.

02

Walk the Paddock

Unlike stadium motorsport, grassroots drifting has an open paddock. You can walk right up to the cars, talk to drivers, and see what makes a drift build tick.

03

Find Your Spot

The action at Jackson Speedway happens close. Find a spot at the fence near a corner and watch cars come in at full lock — loud, smoky, and completely controlled chaos.

04

Eat, Hang, Repeat

Rotating food vendors are on site at events — or pack your own. It's a full day out. Bring the crew, find a spot at the fence, and stay until the last run.
